HRRP Navigator

An autonomous analyst agent for hospital readmission risk

HRRP Navigator investigates CMS hospital readmission penalty data the way a human analyst would: breaking a business question into sub-questions, writing and self-correcting its own SQL, and producing a strategist's recommendation, with every reasoning step logged for review.

The problem

A penalty most hospitals already carry

CMS penalizes hospitals up to 3% of Medicare reimbursement for excess 30-day readmissions. Across this dataset, exposure is less a tail risk and more a baseline condition most health systems are managing right now.

What it found

Key findings

•2.8% of hospitals carry the heaviest penalty burden, while 1,339 sit safely below risk; exposure is concentrated, not evenly spread.
•Star rating is a clean, consistent signal: excess readmission ratio drops steadily from 1.048 to 0.966 as rating improves.
•Single-specialty surgical hospitals rank worst, but only because they're measured on one condition; specialty status itself isn't the problem.
•Ownership type explains little variance (0.953–1.017), not the lever it first appears to be.
•Colorado and Oklahoma show the highest average excess ratios, and are worth a dedicated regional review.
